# Acme Pro Subscription

## Plans

Acme Pro costs $9/month or $89/year (paid annually, a ~17% discount versus
monthly). It includes:

- The extended 45-day hardware return window (see the handbook's returns
  section).
- Priority placement in the support queue.
- Two free firmware-flash sessions per year with a technician, for customers
  uncomfortable running the Acme Recovery Tool themselves.

## Cancellation

Customers can cancel any time from **Account > Subscription**. Cancellation
takes effect at the end of the current billing period; monthly plans are not
refunded for partial months.

## Billing Disputes

- Annual-plan cancellations within the first 14 days receive a full refund.
- Annual-plan cancellations after day 14 receive a prorated refund for whole
  unused months remaining on the term.
- Monthly plans are never prorated — this is a frequent point of customer
  confusion and is worth stating plainly rather than hedging.
- A double charge from a failed payment retry is refunded automatically
  within 3 business days; if it hasn't posted by then, escalate to Manager
  rather than promising a specific date.
