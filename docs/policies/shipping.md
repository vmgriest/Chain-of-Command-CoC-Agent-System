# Shipping Policy

Reference document for Support Manager and above. Covers domestic and
international shipping, plus the delayed-shipment credit.

## Domestic Shipping

Domestic (US) orders ship within 2 business days of purchase.

- **FastShip Ground** — 5-7 business days transit, included free on all orders.
- **FastShip Express** — 2-day transit, $15 flat fee, available at checkout.

Tracking numbers are emailed automatically once a label is generated; a
customer who hasn't received one after 2 business days should be checked for
a payment hold before anything else.

## International Shipping

International orders (Canada, UK, EU, Australia) ship via GlobalPost and take
10-15 business days to arrive. Customs duties and import taxes are the
customer's responsibility and are not included in the order total.

Acme Robotics does not currently ship to addresses outside these regions.

## Delayed Shipments

An order that ships more than 3 business days past its estimated ship date
qualifies for a $10 shipping credit, applied automatically to the customer's
account at fulfillment — no request is required. If a customer asks about a
delay and the credit hasn't posted, check the fulfillment queue before
promising anything further; do not offer a refund on shipping beyond the $10
credit without Manager approval.
