Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my order?

Visit acme.com/track and enter your order number. Order numbers are in the format ORD-XXXXXXX (seven digits) and appear in your confirmation email.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — Acme Robotics ships to Canada, the UK, the EU, and Australia via GlobalPost. See the Shipping Policy document for transit times and customs details.

My RoboArm won't update its firmware. What do I do?

Firmware updates should be applied over USB-C using the Acme Recovery Tool, not over Wi-Fi. If an update was interrupted mid-flash, the unit may show error code E-317; a technician can walk you through recovery.

Where can I buy replacement parts?

Individual replacement parts (grippers, battery packs, joint motors) are sold at acme.com/parts. Parts installed by anyone other than an authorized technician may affect your warranty — see the Warranty section of the support handbook.
